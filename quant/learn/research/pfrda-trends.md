## Pattern changes stated on pages
- 2022 (ixambee): Paper 1 = 80 Qs, 100 marks, 60 min; Reasoning 20; negative-marking rate not stated on that page.
- 2025 and practicemock pattern page: 80 Qs, 100 marks, 60 min; Eng/Quant/Reasoning/GA 20 each, 25 marks each; negative marking "1/4 mark per wrong answer" (practicemock; its "-0.3125" figure is the summariser's own arithmetic, not a page quote - UNVERIFIED).
- 2026 (oliveboard, edutap, oliveboard PYP page): 90 Qs, 100 marks, 60 min; Reasoning 20 -> 30; Quant stays 20 Qs / 25 marks; negative marking 0.25 per wrong answer. Notified Phase 1 for 2026 (not yet analysed).
- 2020 -> 2021+: five-section format vs two-paper format (ixambee cut-off page, thin evidence).

## What the evidence says (no overclaiming)
- Only two years (2022, 2025) have usable topic counts, plus a doubtful 2020 entry. Two data points is not a trend.
- DI appears in both: 5 in 2022; 5-8 in 2025 (sources differ). Highest-counted topic in the verified years.
- Number Series was 5 (or 4) in 2022 and not reported in 2025 by any page (oliveboard explicitly says none; others list none). Quadratic Equations: 5 in 2025, not listed in 2022 counts. So the series-vs-quadratic slot may alternate, but that is two observations only.
- Arithmetic one-liners (Partnership, SI/CI, TSD, Ages, Average, Time&Work) appear mostly as 1-3 each.
- Quant has been described as easy/moderate in both years; 2025 "good attempt" of 10+ is a single-source claim (edutap).
- Quant is 20 Qs / 25 marks in 2022, 2025 and the 2026 pattern; the 2026 change affects Reasoning only.
- Nothing here predicts 2026 topic mix; aggregate counts come from memory-based coaching analyses, not official data.
