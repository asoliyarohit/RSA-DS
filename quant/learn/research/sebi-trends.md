## What the evidence says (not over-claimed)
- Data Interpretation (tabular, caselet, line graph) is the biggest block wherever a count is given: 7-9 of 20 in 2024/2025 (sources differ), 5+3 in 2022 per ixambee, and 3 sets of 5 plus a Venn caselet in the 40-Q 2018-style paper. Several pages say DI is about half the section.
- Calculation-style fixed formats recur: wrong/missing number series (3-5 Qs in 2022, 2024, 2025) and either quadratic equations (2-4 in 2022/2024, 0 in 2025) or approximation (5 in 2025, 0 in 2024 per edutap). Which of these appears changes year to year, so one year's mix is a weak predictor.
- Arithmetic word problems are a minority from 2024 onward (about 5-6 in 2025, a handful of single questions in 2024), but ixambee gives 8-10 for 2022. Topics named: CI/SI, mixture and alligation, profit and loss, ages, mensuration, number system.
- Difficulty is described as easy-to-moderate in 2022 and mostly moderate in 2024-2026; the 2024 labels conflict.
- Year coverage is thin: only 2022, 2024 and the Jan-2026 exam have fetched topic counts. 2018-2021 and 2023 are gaps. Good-attempt and sectional cut-off data are almost absent. Do not treat these counts as a reliable blueprint; they are secondary, memory-based reports that disagree with one another.
