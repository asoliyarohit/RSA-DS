## Pattern changes noted
- DI is the largest block every year found: 14 (2017), 15 (2023, c4s) or 20 (2023, bankersadda), 15-16 (2024). DI sets of 5 Q each appear (3 sets 2024; 4 sets 2023 per bankersadda).
- Quadratic: 3 (2017), 3 or 0 (2023, conflicting), 3-4 (2024).
- Cut-offs rose: 69 -> 87.25 -> 109.50 (c4s, consistent series).
- 2023 vs 2024 shift per bankersadda snippets: 2023 had 5 missing-series Q; 2024 only 2 (pw.live set).

## What the evidence says (cautious)
- Only 3 papers (2017, 2023, 2024) have any topic data; that is thin. Memory-based counts disagree between coaching sites by several questions, so treat counts as +/-3.
- Across all sources, Data Interpretation is the biggest topic (about 14-20 of 40), then arithmetic word problems (about 10-13), then simplification/approximation (4-6), series (2-5), quadratic (0-4). Data sufficiency, probability, mensuration are 0-2.
- Difficulty is described as easy to moderate; Quant good attempts reported at 26-30 (one outlier 30-35).
- No cut-off or topic data for 2018-2022 or 2025, so no trend claims can be made for them.
