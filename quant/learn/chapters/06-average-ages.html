Average and Ages

Lesson 1 · Average, weighted average, evenly spaced numbers

In one line: an average is the equal share you get when the total is levelled out.

1Picture this

Five friends in Pune put their chai money on the table: ₹40, ₹50, ₹60, ₹30, ₹70. Total ₹250. Now share it out equally: each gets ₹50. That equal share is the average.

Equal share ₹50 each

Average = the equal share if everything were levelled out.

2The idea, one tiny step at a time

Add up all the values. That is the sum.
Count the values. Call it n.
Divide: sum ÷ n. That is the average.
Turn it round: sum = average × n. Many questions need this form.
If groups have different sizes, the bigger group pulls the average more. That is weighted.
For evenly spaced numbers (2, 4, 6, 8 or 23, 25, 27) the average sits exactly in the middle.

3The rules (now that you get it)

Average = Sum ÷ n  ·  Sum = Average × n
Weighted average = (n₁×A₁ + n₂×A₂) ÷ (n₁ + n₂)
Evenly spaced numbers (arithmetic progression): average = (first + last) ÷ 2. First n natural numbers: average = (n+1) ÷ 2.

Mean definition: Math Is Fun "Central Measures". Weighted mean: Wikipedia "Weighted arithmetic mean". AP average: Wikipedia "Arithmetic progression". The (n+1)÷2 form follows by putting first = 1, last = n.

4Worked examples, one tap per step

Class A (20 students) averages 60 marks, Class B (30 students) averages 70. Average of all 50?

Step 1. Class A: 20 students, average 60. Sum = 20 × 60 = 1200.
Step 2. Class B: 30 students, average 70. Sum = 30 × 70 = 2100.
Step 3. Together: sum = 3300, students = 50.
Step 4. 3300 ÷ 50 = 66. Answer: 66.

Sum of 5 consecutive odd numbers is 135. Find the largest.

Step 1. Five consecutive odd numbers add to 135. Average = 135 ÷ 5 = 27.
Step 2. In evenly spaced numbers the average is the middle one. Middle = 27.
Step 3. Steps of 2 either side: 23, 25, 27, 29, 31.
Step 4. Check: 23+25+27+29+31 = 135. Largest = 31.

5The trap

Same data: averages 60 and 70.
Wrong move: (60 + 70) ÷ 2 = 65. It ignores that B has more students.
Right: 3300 ÷ 50 = 66. Always rebuild the sums first, then divide by the total count.

6Mental-math shortcut: guess a middle value

Pick a round guess near the numbers, add only the gaps, then correct the guess.

Use it. Average of 48, 52, 55, 45, 50. Guess 50. Gaps: −2, +2, +5, −5, 0. They add to 0, so the average is 50. If gaps added to +10 over 5 numbers, add 10 ÷ 5 = 2 to the guess.

Lesson 2 · When an item is added, removed or replaced

In one line: work in totals, because an average is just total ÷ count.

1Picture this

A cricket team of 10 averages 40 runs. An 11th player walks in and the team average becomes 42. The newcomer did not score 42. He scored enough to lift all 11 players up by 2 each, plus his own 40 baseline.

11 players × 2 extrahis 40

Extra runs needed: 11 × 2 = 22. So he scored 40 + 22 = 62.

2The idea, one tiny step at a time

An average change is a change in the total, shared over everybody.
Total = average × n. Work in totals, not in averages.
Adding a person: new total = old total + new item.
Removing a person: new total = old total − that item.
Replacing: only the difference between new and old item matters.
Shortcut: change in total = change in average × number of items now.

3The rules (now that you get it)

Add 1 item to n items (average A to A′): item = A + (n+1) × (A′ − A)
Replace 1 of n items (average changes by d): new item = old item + n × d
Remove 1 of n items, rest average B: removed = n × A − (n−1) × B

All three come from Sum = Average × n (Math Is Fun "Central Measures"): derived, Python-checked.

4Worked examples, one tap per step

Average of 10 numbers is 40. An 11th is added; average becomes 42. Find it.

Step 1. Old total = 10 × 40 = 400.
Step 2. New total = 11 × 42 = 462.
Step 3. New item = 462 − 400 = 62.
Step 4. Shortcut: 40 + 11 × 2 = 62. Same. Answer: 62.

8 people average 30 years. A 24-year-old is replaced; average becomes 32. Age of the newcomer?

Step 1. 8 people, average 30. Total = 240.
Step 2. After the swap, average 32. Total = 8 × 32 = 256.
Step 3. Total rose by 16. So new person is 16 more than the one who left.
Step 4. 24 + 16 = 40. Check: (240 − 24 + 40) ÷ 8 = 32.

Average of 6 numbers is 25. One is removed and the average of the rest is 24. Which was removed?

Step 1. 6 numbers, average 25. Total = 150.
Step 2. Remove one; the other 5 average 24. Their total = 120.
Step 3. Removed item = 150 − 120 = 30.

5The trap

Average of 10 is 40; adding an item lifts it to 42.
Wrong move: "so the new item is 42". Then the average is only 442 ÷ 11 ≈ 40.2, not 42.
Right: it must cover the lift for all 11 people: 40 + 11 × 2 = 62.

6Mental-math shortcut: lift × people

Say it as a sentence: "old average, plus the lift times the NEW count."

Use it. 9 numbers average 20; a 10th lifts it to 22. Item = 20 + 10 × 2 = 40. For a fall, subtract: if the average falls by 1 when the 8th item comes in at old average 30, item = 30 − 8 × 1 = 22.

Say the answer out loud before you tap Show.

Lesson 3 · Ages

In one line: everybody ages at the same speed, so the gap stays fixed and the ratio does not.

1Picture this

Ravi is 30 and his son Aman is 6. In 10 years Ravi is 40 and Aman is 16. The gap was 24 years then and is 24 years later. Everyone gets older at the same speed, so the gap never changes.

Ravi 30 → 40
Aman 6 → 16

Gap = 24 now, 24 ago, 24 later.

2The idea, one tiny step at a time

Pick a letter for the unknown present age, usually the younger person: x.
Write the other person using the clue: "3 times" gives 3x.
"After t years": add t to every person. "t years ago": subtract t from every person.
Turn the sentence about the future or past into one equation.
Solve, then put the answer back into the question to check.
Ratio 5:3 means ages are 5k and 3k for some k. Find k, not the ages directly.

3The rules (now that you get it)

Age after t years = age now + t  ·  Age t years ago = age now − t
Difference between two ages is constant. Sum of n ages changes by n × t.
Ratio a:b means ages are ak and bk. Add or subtract t on each age, then apply the new ratio.

Ratio meaning (scaling both parts by the same number): Math Is Fun "Ratios". Fixed gap and group total moving by n × t: derived, Python-checked.

4Worked examples, one tap per step

Father is 3 times his son's age. After 12 years he will be twice as old as his son. Present ages?

Step 1. Son = x, father = 3x.
Step 2. After 12 years: son x + 12, father 3x + 12.
Step 3. Father is then twice the son: 3x + 12 = 2(x + 12).
Step 4. 3x + 12 = 2x + 24, so x = 12. Son 12, father 36.
Step 5. Check: after 12 years 48 and 24, and 48 = 2 × 24. Answer: son 12, father 36.

Ratio of A and B's ages is 5:3. After 6 years it will be 7:5. Present ages?

Step 1. Ages are 5k and 3k.
Step 2. After 6 years: (5k + 6) and (3k + 6) are in ratio 7:5.
Step 3. Cross-multiply: 5(5k + 6) = 7(3k + 6).
Step 4. 25k + 30 = 21k + 42, so 4k = 12, k = 3.
Step 5. Ages now: 15 and 9. Check: 21 : 15 = 7 : 5. Answer: 15 and 9.

5The trap

A and B are 15 and 9 now (ratio 5:3).
Wrong move: add 6 to the ratio parts: (5+6) : (3+6) = 11:9.
Right: add 6 to the real ages: 21 : 15 = 7:5. Ratio parts are not ages, so you cannot add years to them.

6Mental-math shortcut: gap and totals

Two facts solve many questions without algebra: the gap is fixed, and a group's total age moves by (people × years).

Use it. Ratio 4:3 and gap 8 years. The gap is 1 part, so 1 part = 8. Younger = 3 × 8 = 24. For a group: 3 people, average 20 now. After 5 years the average is 25, because everyone gains 5.

Say the answer out loud before you tap Show.
