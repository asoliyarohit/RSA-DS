Daily Practice

1How to use this (10 to 15 minutes)

  • Same time every day. 10 to 15 minutes. Do one block of 5, or all four blocks if you have time.
  • Pen and paper ready. Write every step before you tap an answer. Never hold two numbers in your head.
  • Skip is allowed. A skip counts 0. Guess only if you can cross out options first (see the 20% rule below).
  • After a wrong answer, read the trap line. It names the mistake that gives that wrong number.
  • Next day, tap "Redo my errors" first. Then do the new set.

Same date gives the same 20 questions, so you can come back to them. "Practice again" makes a new set.

2Today's set

Order: quadratics and simplification, then series, then table (DI) questions, then word problems. Blocks of 5 with a short break.

Block done

Stand up, drink a glass of water, then come back.

Break-even 20%: a wrong answer costs 0.3125 and a right one earns 1.25. A guess pays off only if your chance of being right is above 0.3125 ÷ (1.25 + 0.3125) = 20%. Blind guessing on 5 options is exactly 20%, so it gains nothing on average. Cross out options first.

3How the score works

Right +1.25 · Wrong −0.3125 · Skip 0

This is the official PFRDA Phase I marking: Quant has 20 questions for 25 marks, and a wrong answer loses one quarter of the marks of that question.

The topic mix (about 6 table questions, 3 quadratic, 3 series, 2 simplification, 6 word problems) is our choice, based on what memory-based exam analyses reported for 2022 and 2025. It is not an official syllabus.

Memorise this

2. % change: divide by the OLD value.
3. Successive % changes multiply, they never add.
4. Speed in km/h × 5/18 = m/s.
5. Guess only above 20% chance.
