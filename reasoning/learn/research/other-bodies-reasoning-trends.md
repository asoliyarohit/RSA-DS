## 4. Coaching tips (all tagged: coaching opinion)

- Practicemock RBI strategy https://www.practicemock.com/blog/rbi-grade-b-reasoning-phase-1-preparation-strategy/ (coaching opinion): attribute "toppers" claim "don't waste time on a single puzzle"; start with 1-2 puzzles where 3-4 clues decode fast; then syllogism/inequality; coding-decoding; misc as confidence boosters. Budget given for 40 min: puzzles/seating 20-25 min for 2-3 solvable sets; syllogism/inequality 8-10; coding 4-5; misc 3-5. "If the logic is not visible in 1 to 2 minutes, skip." Use diagrams/tables; abandon input-output sets with unclear logic; 0.25 negative marking so accuracy first. (Note: the "40 minutes" is that page's claim, not verified against official pattern.)
- Oliveboard SEBI https://www.oliveboard.in/blog/sebi-grade-a-reasoning-preparation/ (coaching opinion): order = inequality, syllogism, direction, blood relations first; coding/input-output mid; complex seating/floor last; give up a puzzle not set up within 3-4 min; Venn/rule elimination for syllogism.
- C4S IRDAI https://c4scourses.in/blog/irda-am-phase-1-analysis-based-pyq/ (coaching opinion): solve misc (syllogism, blood relation, direction) before puzzles because puzzles consume time.
- Practicemock IRDAI https://www.practicemock.com/blog/irdai-assistant-manager-preparation-strategy-for-reasoning/ (coaching opinion): attempt easier questions first; generic otherwise; no elimination detail.
- Practicemock SEBI https://www.practicemock.com/blog/sebi-grade-a-2024-reasoning-preparation-strategy/ (coaching opinion): says puzzles/seating are most important scoring topics, implying them first; no time budget (contradicts Oliveboard order).
- Practicemock RBI puzzles https://www.practicemock.com/blog/how-to-master-puzzles-for-rbi-grade-b-exam/ : no skip advice; draw tables/diagrams; timed practice.
- Practicemock RBI scoring topics https://www.practicemock.com/blog/rbi-grade-b-2024-phase-1-most-scoring-topics-for-reasoning/ : claims 15 accurate attempts can yield 15-20 marks; gives no sequencing.
- Search snippet only (not fetched): SEBI tips from Oliveboard/Bankersadda - skip time-consuming questions; accuracy over count. UNVERIFIED detail.
- Not found: any named topper's own attempt order for Reasoning.

## 5. What the evidence says (without overclaiming)

- Puzzle + seating is the largest block in every body: IRDAI roughly 15-20 of 40 (sources vary), SEBI roughly 8-15 of 20 depending on year/source (SEBI 2025: 12; 2024: 12), RBI roughly 25-30 of 60 (every source that gives it).
- Remaining marks come from short topics (syllogism, blood relation, coding, direction, inequality, input-output, data sufficiency, critical reasoning). Their mix changes year to year (edutap SEBI note says no fixed topic pattern). SEBI 2024 had no syllogism/inequality; RBI Input-Output was 5 every year in edutap's table; Critical Reasoning is 8-12 in RBI 2021-2026.
- Difficulty labels disagree across sources and shifts; the most consistent reading is "moderate" with time-heavy sets rather than conceptually hard ones. RBI is the one described as hardest (moderate to difficult in 2021, 2022, 2024-edutap).
- Good-attempt figures are not comparable across years/sources (RBI 12-18 up to 27-32; IRDAI 25-31 of 40; SEBI 15-17 of 20 for one 2026 shift).
- Tips converge on: do short topics first or skip a puzzle that is not cracking within about 1-4 minutes; tables/diagrams. They conflict on whether puzzles go first. All tips are coaching opinion; none is tested against outcome data here.
- Gaps: IRDAI 2018-2022, 2025; SEBI 2018-2021, 2023 (verified); RBI 2017-2020 (no verified counts). Do not infer trends before 2021 for RBI or before 2022 for SEBI/IRDAI from this file.
