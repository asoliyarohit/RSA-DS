# Retro: PFRDA Reasoning Lab
## What worked
- Same pipeline as Quant (write -> independent fact-check -> student review -> integration), reused shell and briefs.
- Brute force as ground truth: every puzzle, syllogism, inequality, input-output, direction and family-tree answer was re-solved from scratch by an independent checker. Checkers still found real flaws: puzzles with two valid solutions (ch10), odd-one-out and analogy questions with two defensible answers (ch13, mock A), a false syllogism shortcut (ch03), an impossible family example (ch05), wrong stems with no set-up sentence (ch10), unreproducible verification counts (ch03, ch12).
- Independent check of my own priority chapter corrected misattributed coaching tips and wrong ranges (01b).
## What did not work
- Writers' own reports were optimistic; the second pair of eyes was essential.
- Student reviewers added hand-checked arithmetic only; the arithmetic scan found 0 real errors but covers simple statements only.
- Shared scratchpad collisions between writers.
- The negative-marking figure was a point of confusion: derivation (1/4 of 1.25 or of 0.8333) versus coaching pages' flat -0.25. Resolved by the user's direction; flagged in the guide.
