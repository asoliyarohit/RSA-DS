Safety Net: what to do when you blank

In one line: when a question freezes you, you do not fight it. You run a fixed 3-step routine: cross out, decide, move on.

Label: the maths here is checked in Python. The advice (skip rules, time plans, reset steps) is our suggested strategy, not an official fact. Marks used: +0.8333 per right, −0.25 per wrong (flat, as this guide assumes).

Lesson 1 · Elimination and the guess rule

In one line: a guess is only worth it when your chance of being right is above about 23%.

1Picture this

You are in a Pune chai shop with four snacks on the menu and you have no idea what is good. You cannot taste them first. Crossing out the two you already know are stale leaves two. Now picking one is a fair bet. Exam options work the same way: every option you can prove wrong makes the remaining guess better.

2The idea, one tiny step at a time

Each option is either right or wrong. Only one is right.
Process of elimination means finding the answer by excluding the options that cannot be it.
