trend_crisis - diversified cross-asset LONG/SHORT trend / crisis-alpha book, daily capture via CLOSE and OPEN slots
=====================================================================================================================
Dev data only (<= 2022-12-31). --final never run. Judge and analyst/ untouched. N_TRIALS = 122 (tally at the end).
Scope (owner change mid-round): indices, commodities and single stocks only; no crypto, no FX, no rates ETFs.

0. USER HYPOTHESIS UNDER TEST
   "A short book makes money when markets tank, so direction is irrelevant; a long/short trend book earns in 2008,
   2020 Q1 and 2022 as well as in bull years." We test it net of the judge's per-instrument costs, with the only slots
   available (OPEN: open->close with stop; CLOSE: close->next open), on dev data, with pre-registered hypotheses.

1. PRE-REGISTERED HYPOTHESES AND VERDICTS
   H1 TSMOM (sign of 12-1m return, Moskowitz-Ooi-Pedersen 2012; also 1/3/6/12m vote) held each night via CLOSE on
      cheap classes beats the per-slot cost.                                    -> REJECTED in every class (table A).
   H2 Same, held each day via OPEN (intraday leg).                               -> REJECTED in every class (table A).
   H3 The SHORT side of that book earns in 2008 / 2020Q1 / 2022 net of costs.    -> Gross yes, net ~0 for indices:
      index CLOSE short 2008 +8.2 bps net (t~1.7 with the vote signal), 2022 +4.1 bps net (t~0.3), 2000-02 +4.6.
      2020Q1: 12-1m momentum was still LONG (short share 4%) -> -93 bps/slot. The vote signal flipped faster: +75 bps
      on shorts, but only ~140 short-slots. Shorts do not pay in bull years (-3 to -10 bps net). See tables A/B.
   H4 Selectivity (|signal| strength, relative vol, full lookback agreement, VIX>25 & term inversion, breadth) lifts
      daily TSMOM above cost.                                                    -> REJECTED. Best cell: index CLOSE in
      crisis regime +5.6 bps net, t 1.17, n 2345; everything else negative. Commodity OPEN shorts in crisis +24 bps
      t 2.06 n 1085 is one cell out of ~50 (2008 and 2020 oil) and is not credible after the trial count.
   H5 Purged walk-forward ridge stacking of 18 trend/reversal/regime features (refit each 1 Jan, 21-day embargo, pooled
      vol-normalised target, trade only when |E[ret]| > k x cost) earns after cost.
      -> CLOSE leg positive on dev but NOT VALIDATED by the judge (t 1.75, DSR 0.20, CI95 [-0.7, +12.8] bps).
         OPEN leg negative at every k. And what the model learned is NOT trend: the three largest coefficients are
         IBS (-), last-month return (-) and 12-1m momentum (+) = short-horizon reversal plus slow trend. Fast trend
         features (EMA 8/24, 16/48, 1m z) carry negative or ~0 weight for the next-night return.

6. WHAT WAS TRIED THAT DID NOT WORK (all counted in N_TRIALS)
   - Yahoo continuous futures (GC, CL, BZ, NG, SI, HG) looked best in the stacking (NG=F +34 bps/trade) but their
     close->open return embeds contract ROLL gaps (NG=F averages +12.9 bps/night, -6.5 intraday: a contango artefact).
     A CFD never pays or receives the roll gap, so this is fake edge; futures were dropped from the frozen strategy.
   - Single stocks: dead at 66 bps round trip (-65 bps/slot everywhere).
   - OPEN leg: negative for every signal, class and filter; the stop (2.5 ATR) does not help.
   - Crisis filter (VIX>25 and VIX/VIX3M>1) short-only on indices: +3.7 bps net, t 0.68, n 2021 -> not usable.
   - Breadth gating, |z| strength deciles, relative-vol deciles: monotone nowhere, all negative.
   - Stacking per class instead of pooled: similar; pooled chosen a priori for fewer parameters.

7. KNOWN WEAKNESSES (top three first)
   1. The surviving edge sits almost entirely in EWJ/EWU/FEZ overnight (US close -> US open spans the Tokyo/London
      sessions). The judge prices them as 20x / 4 bps "major index" CFDs. The real Revolut Japan 225 / UK 100 CFDs
      trade almost around the clock, so a "US-close to US-open" position in them is a different trade with a
      different price path; EWJ/EWU themselves would be 5x / 66 bps equity CFDs. The number may not be tradable.
   2. Decay: 2006-2014 +9.2 bps t 2.25; 2015-2022 -0.9 bps t -0.14. The 2020 Q1 loss (-16%) is the worst drawdown
      and came from longs; nothing in the feature set protected against it.
   3. Multiple testing: 122 trials, DSR 0.20, CI95 straddles zero. Judge-visible numbers (6 bps/slot) are below the
      15 bps daily-bar resolution floor noted in RESULTS.md. The required edge for the 10y goal is ~160 bps/slot at
      this slot rate and sizing; we are at 4% of it.
   Also: closes stand in for 15:55 fills; overnight gap risk is unstopped (worst -1374 bps on a single trade); GLD
   proxies gold at 7 bps; costs on foreign-index CFDs are the judge's assumption, not measured quotes.

8. CONCLUSION FOR THE USER
   A diversified long/short trend/crisis book, implemented in the only slots this account has, does not earn after
   costs on 2001-2022 data. Its short side made money in 2008 and 2022 (about +26 and +13 bps per short trade) but lost
   or sat out in 2020 Q1 and bleeds in bull years; net over everything the book is +6 bps/slot with t 1.75 and a
   deflated Sharpe of 0.2, which the judge correctly refuses. The user's premise ("direction is irrelevant because the
   short book pays when markets tank") is not supported: shorts help in slow bears and fail in fast crashes, and
   the per-slot cost eats the daily capture of any slow trend signal. 1k -> 100k remains unsupported (P = 0%).
