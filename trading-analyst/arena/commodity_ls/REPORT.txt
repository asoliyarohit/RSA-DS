  contain contract-roll jumps. CLOSE slots on futures would book roll artifacts as P&L -> futures traded OPEN only.
  Features use a roll-free price = cumulative open->close returns (futures) / close-to-close (GLD).
- GLD is the clean gold proxy (real 9:30 opens; judge maps it to gold CFD 20x, 7 bp round trip).
- SLV/USO/UNG/DBC get stock-CFD costs (25 bp/side) in the judge -> uneconomic, excluded.
- CL=F printed a NEGATIVE close on 2020-04-20. The judge books a short that day at +312% of notional (+3,121 bp). That
  single artifact turned H1 from -1.8 bp to +1.0 bp/slot. CL=F was removed from the shipped universe (BZ=F keeps oil);
  this post-hoc change makes results MORE conservative and is counted as a trial.
Final universe: GLD, BZ=F, NG=F, HO=F, RB=F, ZC=F, ZW=F, ZS=F. Trades 2008-2022. Stop = 1.5 x 60d std of open->close
returns (median 2.4%). Costs per judge: futures 12 bp round trip at 10x, GLD 7 bp at 20x.

4. Goal arithmetic
   Even 10x leverage and 252 slots/yr need ~35 bp/slot NET for P(1k->100k in 10y) >= 50%. Best gross edge found is ~12 bp,
   i.e. 3x short before costs. 1k->100k is not supported by any commodity rule tested here.

5. Top weaknesses / caveats
   1) No net edge: gross trend edge (~10-12 bp) == assumed CFD cost (12 bp); conclusion hinges on unverified Revolut
      commodity spreads (assumed 8 bp + 2x2 bp slippage). If real costs were ~4 bp, H1/H3 would be ~+8 bp gross-ish -
      still far below the 35 bp goal and not significant.
   2) Data quality: Yahoo continuous futures have stale opens (metals unusable), roll gaps, an ambiguous open time
      (probably the 18:00 ET Globex open vs 14:30 settlement close) and the negative-WTI print. A real CFD tracks a
      different contract with its own roll schedule; fills here are approximations.
   3) Universe/selection: 8 instruments chosen after a data audit; Revolut may not offer heating oil, gasoline or grains
      as CFDs. CL=F removal was post-hoc (conservative). Term structure only via a noisy roll-gap proxy (H5 failed);
      real curve data (CFTC/exchange settlements) was not reachable free with clean history.
